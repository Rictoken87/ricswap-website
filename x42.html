x42 is not a rebase token. Your token count stays the same; each x42 becomes worth more 42.

Does my x42 balance go up?

No. x42 is price-appreciating, like rETH. Rewards raise the exchange rate, not your token count — so it works in AMMs and wallets without rebase hooks.

Where does the 42 go?

The pool is the only Cosmos delegator. Native 42 is routed to a curated Infinite Drive validator set. It never sits with an admin key. Admin is a 72-hour timelock.

How long to unstake?

21 days — the chain’s unbonding time. Your x42 is burned when you request; you claim native 42 when the ticket matures. Instant exit is 0.5% and only fills when someone else deposits.

What if a validator is slashed?

Remaining x42 holders socialize the NAV drop. Unstake tickets keep their face value. Withdrawals can pause if backing cannot cover the queue.
