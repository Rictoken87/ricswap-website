What RicSwap is

  • A small DEX and CleanToken launchpad on Shibarium (chain 109). Swap fee 0.3% to LPs. Launch fee in RIC.

What RicSwap is not

  • Not investment advice. Nothing here is an offer to sell securities, a promise of profit, or a recommendation to buy RIC, 42, x42, or any launched token.
  • Not a bank or custodian. If you send tokens to the wrong chain or a fake address, they are gone.
  • Not Uniswap-scale. Liquidity can be thin. Price impact can be large. That is not hidden TVL — it is a small venue.
