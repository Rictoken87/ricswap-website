WHY HOLD RIC?

Max supply is fixed at 25,000,000. 4,000,000 are already permanently burned, leaving 21,000,000 in effective circulation. Product fees keep removing more RIC from the market.

  • ✓ Launch access — 100 RIC to create a fair token
  • ✓ Verify access — 100 RIC for explorer source verify
  • ✓ Team burn — 4,000,000 RIC to 0x…dEaD
  • ✓ Fee burns — 50% of every launch & verify fee
  • ✓ Treasury — 50% of fees for ecosystem

Note: on-chain totalSupply() may still read 25,000,000; burned RIC sits at the dead address and cannot move. Circulating = 21,000,000 after the 4,000,000 burn (before other locks).
