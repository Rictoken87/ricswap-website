How to use — click to show

In Rabby, set this website to Infinite Drive (421018) first. If the wallet asks for ETH, you are on the wrong chain. Do not send 42 to the announcer or registry.

You — receive

  1. Click Connect wallet.
  2. Open the Receive tab.
  3. Click Sign EIP-712 to unlock. The wallet must show domain Infinite Drive Stealth. This is not a login and not a transfer.
  4. Copy your public string: st:drive:0x…
  5. Send that whole string to your friend (chat, email, etc.).

Friend — pay

  1. Connect on Infinite Drive (421018).
  2. Open the Pay tab.
  3. Paste the full st:drive:0x… into Recipient.
  4. Enter the amount of 42. Leave the stipend as quoted. Leave metadata unchecked.
  5. Click Send 42 + announce. Confirm two popups: send 42, then announce with value 0.

You — scan

  1. Same wallet as Receive. Sign EIP-712 again if you closed the tab.
  2. Open Scan & sweep.
  3. From block: 0 (last ~20,000 blocks). Leave Withdraw to empty.
  4. Click Scan (view-only). You should see the 42 on a one-time address — not in your everyday Rabby balance.

You — sweep (optional)

  1. Check Enable sweep.
  2. Withdraw to: an 0x you control. Use a new wallet if you do not want the payment tied to your public address. The page blocks sweeping to the connected wallet.
  3. On the row with the 42, click sweep.

42 never lands in your usual public wallet until you sweep. Sweeping to a known wallet makes that payment traceable. Stealth means “don’t pay the public inbox,” not “untraceable cash.”
